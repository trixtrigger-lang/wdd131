Kenyan Street Bites

Site Name

Kenyan Street Bites represents the vibrant and flavorful street food culture of Kenya.

Site Purpose

The website provides information about popular Kenyan street foods, their preparation, and where to find them.

Scenarios

  • Where can I find the best nyama choma?
  • What affordable street foods can I try in Nairobi?
  • How is mutura prepared?
